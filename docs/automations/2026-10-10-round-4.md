## Deviations

- Non-host target clippy is not applicable to `assistant-task-engine`: it depends on
  `assistant-storage` and therefore the SQLite C build chain. ADR-0045 limits the
  `--target` clippy requirement to pure Rust crates. CI covers the three-platform
  matrix.
- Several read-only commands were accidentally issued under `shell=bash` and exited
  127 before any write. They were rerun under `shell=powershell`; no command was
  reported as passed without a successful rerun.
- The diff exceeds the 400-line single-card review budget because the tests and
  governance evidence land together. The work is kept in one round because the cases
  jointly close the same checkpoint / recovery coverage gap.
